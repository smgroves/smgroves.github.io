SCLC Phenospace

Interactive Fig. 3B–C from Wooten, Groves et al. (2019) PLoS Comput Biol

What this shows. Each dot is one of 1,728 GO biological processes enriched in at least one WGCNA gene module from 50 SCLC cell lines (CCLE RNA-seq). Terms are placed with t-SNE on GoSemSim semantic distances, so similar processes sit together. Gray dots are the whole phenospace (Fig. 3B); colored dots are the terms enriched in the selected modules (Fig. 3C), sized by enrichment p-value.
