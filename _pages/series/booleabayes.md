This series goes through why network inference is important in biology. Cell identity can be considered to be the expression of key transcription factors (TF) that regulate each other. This regulation builds a gene regulatory network (GRN). Figuring out which TFs connect to where, and how perturbations to the genes will affect cell identity, is a central problem across biology, from cancer to autoimmune disease to developmental disorders. If you are not familiar with GRN inference, this is a good place to start!
