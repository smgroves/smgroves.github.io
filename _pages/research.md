I study **cancer systems biology** and **biological networks**. I'm a physicist by training and a network scientist by practice: I model high-dimensional biological data to understand how cells change state, from cell plasticity in small cell lung cancer to the physics of chromosome segregation during mitosis. Along the way my group builds software and interactive apps so other researchers can use the same methods on their own data.
