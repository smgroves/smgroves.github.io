blurb: >
  SCLC is a highly aggressive form of lung cancer that initially responds well to treatment but quickly comes back in almost all patients. One reason we think it may come back is a high degree of *phenotypic heterogeneity and plasticity,* meaning the cells are intrinsically able to "shape-shift" in response to drug treatments to avoid them. I build and use computational tools that investigate the underlying mechanisms of SCLC plasticity. My focus is combining ODE modeling, Boolean network simulations, and experiments with collaborators to figure out better ways to categorize and treat this type of cancer.
