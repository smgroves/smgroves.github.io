DS5030: Understanding Uncertainty

Probability and statistics for data science in the UVA MSDS program — distributions, simulation, the bootstrap, likelihood, and Bayesian inference.

DS 5030 is a graduate course in the UVA School of Data Science that picks up the threads of students’ math and probability education and builds a foundation for later work in machine learning and AI, grounded in data. Topics include linear algebra for data science, random variables and expectation, representing distributions (ECDFs, CDFs/PDFs, kernel density estimates), order statistics and extreme events, sampling distributions, the bootstrap and the CLT, joint and conditional densities, Markov chains, maximum likelihood, regression and gradient descent, and Bayesian inference.

Each week has a Tuesday math day (concept-driven lecture and quiz) and a Thursday lab day (a pre-lab bridge notebook, then a coding lab that starts in class). Students fork a template repository and pull new lecture notebooks, labs, and datasets each week; the datasets for every notebook below are in that repo.
